## MobileRead — KOReader subforum (announcement thread)

**Forum:** https://www.mobileread.com/forums/forumdisplay.php?f=276
**Title:** `[Plugin] AnkiKOAi — turn KOReader highlights into Anki cards (AI vocab + memorization)`

```
I built a KOReader plugin called AnkiKOAi that turns reading into Anki flashcards
without leaving your book.

Long-press a word or highlight a passage, pick a card type, and it sends a
finished card to desktop Anki over Wi-Fi via AnkiConnect:

- Wiki Card (AI): term on the front; a Wikipedia/Wiktionary-style article plus
  explore links on the back.
- Vocabulary Card (no AI): KOReader dictionary definition + the passage.
- Memorization (no AI): LPCG-style overlapping step cards plus an optional full
  recitation card, great for poetry and prose.

Everything is configurable on the device (API keys, decks, note types, sync) —
no SSH or file editing on the Kobo. Optional auto-sync to AnkiWeb after sending.
Works on Kobo, Kindle (with KOReader), and the desktop emulator. AI is optional;
you can use multiple providers (DashScope, Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter).

Repo + setup guide: LINK

Feedback and bug reports welcome — latest release: v1.0.9.
```

## Reddit r/Anki

**Subreddit:** https://reddit.com/r/Anki
**Title:** `I read on an e-reader and auto-generate Anki cards from highlights via AnkiConnect`

```
I do most of my reading on an e-ink device and wanted my highlights to become
Anki cards automatically instead of copy-pasting later. So I wrote a KOReader
plugin (AnkiKOAi) that does it over AnkiConnect.

Workflow: long-press a word or highlight a passage in KOReader → choose a card
type → it sends a finished card to desktop Anki on the same Wi-Fi.

Card types:
- Wiki Card (AI): term + a Wikipedia/Wiktionary-style article with explore links.
- Vocabulary Card (no AI): dictionary definition + the sentence it came from.
- Memorization (no AI): LPCG-style step cards + a full recitation card for
  poetry/prose, organized into Memorize::Book::page subdecks.

It auto-syncs to AnkiWeb after sending if you want, and all settings live on the
device. AI is optional and supports DashScope/Gemini/OpenAI/OpenRouter.

It's free and open source (MIT): LINK

Happy to answer setup questions — you do need AnkiConnect running and a one-time
note-type setup, which the guide walks through.
```
